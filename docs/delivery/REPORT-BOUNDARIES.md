# Report endpoint and figure boundaries

An excluded recording remains an excluded trial. Its expected mouse remains held for an endpoint requiring complete repeats unless the expected mouse itself was explicitly excluded. Expected, supported, held and excluded counts are separate.

Individual density figures carry their own support warning and source interval. A stored/raw density marked held does not become a supported group result. Group figures include planned phase/day/window/orientation and map cohort denominators. Known fractions and unknown mass are descriptive accepted-producer outputs, not biological accuracy. PATH preserves source aspect with letterboxing and does not bridge unknown frames.

The confirmed training task policy below preserves individual values and actual durations. Never substitute a fixed common duration or zero for a held endpoint.

## Confirmed training task means

Adjacent speed and proximity can be averaged across differing actual training durations only when EVERY expected repeat has supported whole-trial observation, a supported released comparison, and confirmed platform mounting as its termination. Trial values are averaged equally within the actual animal; supported animals are then weighted equally. Group contrasts remain within the same planned training context/protocol and endpoint units. No single common duration is reported for task groups, and differing-duration animals also have a blank common-duration field. Actual individual durations remain explicit in TRIAL_SCALARS.csv; native cards and HTML/CSV explain that these are task-trial means, not fixed-time comparisons.

Incomplete, excluded, unconfirmed or truncated repeats continue to hold their animal endpoint. Differing-duration occupancy and totals remain aggregate-held; their individual supported measurements and durations are retained. Probe windows and fixed-window spatial map eligibility are unchanged. A timeout alone does not license this variable-duration mounting policy; ordinary matched-duration supported results retain their existing route. No p-values, factorial inference or causal memory score is introduced.

Standalone group density and difference figures separate overall context counts from each group's map cohort. A contrast lists only its two groups' expected/included/held/excluded denominators; unrelated groups do not enter those pair labels. Density warnings wrap within a larger standalone figure area.
